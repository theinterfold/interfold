4 Homomorphic statistics

Computed ON the ciphertexts: the homomorphic sum lands in slot 0 and the sum of squares — a GENUINE ct×ct multiplication per participant, relinearized with the committee's joint ceremony key — in slot 1 of ONE output ciphertext. One evaluation, ONE threshold opening; mean, variance and std-dev are derived client-side from the two aggregates. Individual salaries are never decrypted.

5 Threshold decryption & survey result

Seeing the ciphertext on-chain, each committee node computes a decryption share (secret share + smudging noise). Any t+1 = 2 shares reconstruct — the aggregator publishes canonical fixed-point bytes via publishPlaintextOutput. The opening contains exactly TWO aggregate values and nothing else.
