## 1. TL;DR / verdict

- **Constraints are linear in `N·L` for every committee circuit** [M, §2]: at fixed L = 3 the
  ACIR count is `a·N` with `a` = 78 (C1), 50 (C2a), 95 (C2b), 100 (C6), 164 (C8-digit,
  LT = 4) — R² > 0.999 over N = 512…8192. Per (coefficient × limb) that is ≈ 26 (C1), 17
  (C2a), 32 (C2b), 33 (C6), 41 (C8) constraints. C7 is **independent of N** (22 879
  constraints, `MAX_MSG_NON_ZERO_COEFFS = 100` window) — but see §4.4: that window is a
  BFV assumption that CKKS violates.
- **bb prove is cheap and linear** [M]: ≈ 10–17 s per 10⁶ constraints, ≈ 5–6.5 GiB peak RSS
  per 10⁶ constraints, single machine. A 5-M-constraint circuit (C1 at S2) proves in
  ≈ 50 s / 25 GiB.
- **`nargo compile` is the real wall, and it is super-linear** [M]: C1/C6 compile RAM grows
  ≈ 1.9–2.2× per doubling of N (≈ N^1.0–N^1.1), but C2a/C2b/C8 grow **≈ 2.5–2.9× per
  doubling** in RAM and **≈ 3.7× per doubling in time** (≈ N^1.9). At S1, C1/C6 compile
  in ⚠ ~80–100 GiB (measured to N = 16384: 8.4 / 12.6 GiB at L = 3, §2.2); C2a/C2b/C8-digit need ⚠ 100–250 GiB and hours. At S2 nothing compiles
  under ≈ 350 GiB. Compile is one-time per artifact set (rentable), so **the operational
  gate for Track A is prove RAM (≈ 5.5 GiB per 10⁶ constraints [M]), and the artifact-production
  gate is compile RAM** — §3.
- **The relin-key ceremony is the largest proof volume in the pipeline**: `2 · dnum` proofs
  per party (round 1 exists; **round 2 has no circuit anywhere** — specified in §5.3),
  each an `LT = L + k` limb-row circuit. At S1: 18 proofs of ≈ 18–24 M constraints each ⚠;
  at S2: 26 proofs of ≈ 73–97 M each ⚠. This — not decryption — is what makes
  "multiplications" expensive to prove.
- **Fully proven at S1, per party, prove-time only (Track A, one 64-core/256 GiB box)** ⚠:
  DKG once ≈ 12 min (C1 3 + C2a 3 + C2b 5.6 + fold), ceremony once ≈ 9 × (4.7 + 3.6) min ≈ 75 min,
  per decryption ≈ 5.6 min (C6). At S2 ⚠: DKG ≈ 50 min, ceremony ≈ 13 × (19 + 15) min ≈ 7.4 h, per
  decryption ≈ 24 min. Compile is one-time per artifact set, not per E3.
- **Recommendation**: Track A (big circuits on a big box) for every *per-party* committee
  proof at S1 with per-digit C8 (already the shape that exists); Track B (folding) only for
  (i) the per-committee aggregators (needed regardless — one proof per stage on-chain),
  (ii) everything at S2, (iii) browser Greco. §3 argues why; §7 orders the work.

## 3. Big-circuit vs folding

The user's question: *why fold — can't we just run huge circuits on bigger compute?* The
honest answer is "yes at S1 for per-party proofs, no at S2, and folding is needed anyway
for a different reason".

### 3.1 What folding buys and what it does not

Folding (the BFV `c3_fold` / `c6_fold` / `node_fold` / `nodes_fold` / `dkg_aggregator`
pattern under `circuits/bin/recursive_aggregation`) chains many *leaf* Honk proofs into ONE
accumulator proof by verifying the previous accumulator (`verify_honk_proof_non_zk`) and one
new leaf (`verify_honk_proof`) per step, carrying the leaves' public inputs forward in
fixed slots (`c6_fold` keeps `[sk, esm, ct, d] × (T + 1)` columns; `c3_fold` keeps
`N_PARTIES · L_THRESHOLD` slots). Its payoff:

1. **Verification count** — the on-chain / peer verifier checks ONE proof per stage
   (Π_DKG, Π_dec) instead of `n × (#circuits)` leaves. This is the actual reason the BFV
   stack folds: `decryption_aggregator` is what the contract verifies (≈ 3.6 M gas), not
   `T + 1` C6 leaves + C7.
2. **Bounded prover RAM** — each step is a fixed-size recursive-verifier circuit
   (≈ 0.75–2 M constraints regardless of the leaf's size), so a machine that can prove a
   2 M-constraint circuit can fold arbitrarily many leaves.

What it does NOT buy: **total prover CPU**. Folding adds ≈ 5–16 s per step (measured BFV:
`c2ab_fold` 16 s, `c6_fold` step ≈ 5 s, `node_fold` 12 s on an M4 Pro, secure-8192
report) on top of the leaves, which still have to be proven at full size unless the *leaf
itself* is split into kernels (`c3_fold_kernel` / `c6_fold_kernel` are genesis kernels,
not witness splitters — no existing fold splits one witness across steps).

### 3.2 So when do we need to split the witness?

Only when a single leaf does not fit the prover's RAM: `bb prove` RSS ≈ 5.5 GiB per 10⁶
constraints [M]. That is the sizing rule:

| box RAM | largest leaf that proves | S1 leaves that fit | S2 leaves that fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 36 GiB (this laptop) | ≈ 5.5 M | none (C7-widened barely) | none |
| 128 GiB | ≈ 20 M | C1, C2a, C6, C7; **not** C2b (28 M), **not** C8 digits (27 M) | none |
| 256 GiB | ≈ 45 M | everything | none |
| 512 GiB | ≈ 90 M | everything | C1, C2a, C6, C7, C8-r2 digit; **not** C2b (120 M), **not** C8-r1 digit (110 M) |
| 1 TiB | ≈ 180 M | everything | everything |

(Compile RAM is ≤ prove RAM for C1/C6 per §2.3 and ≈ 1–1.5× prove RAM for C2/C8; it is
one-time per artifact set, can be done on a rented box, and the artifact is what ships —
so compile does not gate *operations*, only *artifact production*.)

### 3.3 Two-track plan

**Track A — big circuits on a big box** (per-party committee proofs at S1)

| proof | S1 box | S1 per-proof time ⚠ | per party per E3 | where A breaks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | 128 GiB | 3 min | ×1 | fine |
| C2a + C2b | 256 GiB (C2b 155 GiB) | 3 + 6 min | ×1 each | C2b at n ≥ 10 (× 2) needs 512 GiB |
| C8 r1 + r2, per digit | 256 GiB | 4.7 + 3.6 min | × dnum = 9 → **≈ 75 min** | the volume: 18 proofs per party per ceremony; with 4 boxes ≈ 20 min wall |
| C6 | 128 GiB | 5.6 min | ×1 per decryption | fine |
| C7 (widened) | 128 GiB | 2 min | ×1 per decryption (aggregator) | fine |
| Greco ct0 + ct1 + app (per user) | — | ⚠ ≈ 26 M constraints per leg at S1 (Greco ≈ 40 · N · L, 2 legs) → **minutes, ≈ 140 GiB** | ×1 per user | **browser: impossible** (bb.js caps ≈ 2^21–2^22 gates ≈ 4 M constraints in Chrome); needs server-assisted proving or Track B |

Peer verification load under Track A: every committee member verifies every other
member's leaves — `n − 1` × (C1 + C2a + C2b + 2·dnum C8) at 26 ms each (Honk verify is
size-independent, [M] 0.0 s in every row; the secure-8192 BFV report gives 26 ms) — ≈ 0.6 s
per party per ceremony. Verification is NOT the bottleneck at any n; proof *transport* is
15 KB × proofs, also negligible. Track A's break points are (i) S2 prove RAM (> 350 GiB
per leaf), (ii) the C8 volume × dnum, (iii) browser Greco.

**Track B — folding**, used for:

1. **Per-committee aggregators — required regardless of A/B**: a CKKS `node_fold` (C1 +
   c2ab + one new `c8_fold` column) and a CKKS `decryption_aggregator` (`c6_fold` + C7)
   so the contract verifies one Π_DKG and one Π_dec per E3 exactly as for BFV. The inner
   leaves can be the Track A big proofs (the fold verifies a Honk proof; its size is fixed
   by the leaf's VK, not its constraint count — recursive verification cost is
   ≈ 0.4–0.9 M constraints per verified proof at any leaf size, [M] BFV `c6_fold`
   = 1 verify_honk + 1 verify_honk_non_zk). So B's aggregator layer is **independent of
   whether the leaves are big or kernel-split**.
2. **S2 per-party leaves**: split each leaf into kernel steps over coefficient chunks (§5).
3. **Browser Greco at S1/S2**: kernel-split ct0/ct1 legs proven in bb.js (≤ 4 M constraints
   per step) and folded client-side or server-side.

### 3.4 Recommendation

A-first for S1 committee proofs (existing circuits, existing per-digit C8 shape, no new
Noir except `c8_round2_digit` and the C7 widening; buy/rent one 256 GiB machine per
committee member, or a shared prover service if the threat model allows a party to
outsource its OWN witness — it does not for C2/C6/C8 which carry secrets, so per-party
boxes). B for the aggregators (needed anyway), for S2, and for browser Greco. The plan in
§7 is ordered that way. Folding's headline benefit is verification count; treat "kernel
splitting" as the S2/browser tool, not the default.

### 4.1 Where the CKKS fold public-input layout differs from BFV

- **C1**: BFV `c1_public: [Field; 3]` is `(sk_c, pk_c, e_sm_c)`; CKKS is the same triple,
  but `pk_commitment` is `commit(reverse+center(pk0) ‖ CRP)` (witness builder
  `compute_ckks_pk_commitment_from_share_bytes`). `node_fold`'s `assert(c1_public[0] ==
  c2ab_public[C2A_INNER_START])` / `[2] == C2B_INNER_START` hold unchanged. The node fold
  must ALSO export `c1_public[0]` (`sk_commitment`) because the C8 gate anchors `s_commitment`
  to it — a new tail slot per party.
- **C2a/C2b**: identical layout (`1 + n·L`); L is the CKKS L (18 / 38), so
  `C2_PUBLIC_LEN` = 91 / 191 at n = 5 — `c2ab_fold`'s `[[Field; L_THRESHOLD]; N_PARTIES]`
  is generated per preset already (`configs/<preset>/dkg.nr`), needs a CKKS preset dir.
- **C6**: BFV row is `(sk, esm, ct, domain_hi, domain_lo, d)`; CKKS C6 has the identical
  6-field row (`share_decryption_ckks/src/main.nr`). `c6_fold` reusable verbatim; the
  `d_commitment` column is what `decryption_aggregator` matches against C7's
  `expected_d_commitments`.
- **C7**: BFV C7 public tail is `[d_commitments | party_ids | message[100]]`; CKKS is
  `[d_commitments | party_ids | u_global[MAX]]` — same shape today (100), but `u_global` is a
  *ring element at scale Δ*, not a decoded message: the EVM wrapper must hash it as the
  ciphertext-output commitment rather than read it as plaintext (`CkksFixedPointLib`
  decodes off-circuit). After the §4.4 widening `MAX` becomes N (32768 fields ≈ 1 MiB of
  public inputs — must itself be committed inside the aggregator: `u_global_commitment`
  as the single public output; the aggregator recomputes it from the C7 leaf's public tail).
- **C8** (no BFV counterpart): new `c8_fold` column: per party `(s_c, u_c, share_c[0..dnum],
  r2_share_c[0..dnum])`. `dnum` is a preset constant (9 / 13).
- **Commitment tail / sk commitment**: BFV `dkg_aggregator` exports per-party `(sk_agg,
  esm_agg)` from C4 (share decryption of received shares). CKKS has no C3/C4 today
  (shares travel BFV-encrypted through the unchanged `ThresholdShare` event but the CKKS
  shell publishes empty C3 slots). A fully-proven CKKS DKG therefore needs C3/C4 at the
  *transport* (BFV, wide-512 or a secure transport preset) shape — **not covered by this
  document's measurements**; they are the BFV circuits at the BFV transport preset and scale
  with the transport N, not the CKKS N. Flagged in §7 as a separate item.

### 4.2 Invariants the fold must preserve (`agent/INVARIANTS.md`)

- "Committee-wide CKKS decisions are pure functions of the on-chain `ParamSet`" — the fold

- "C8 gate: a hybrid round-1 share is aggregated only after its `dnum` digit proofs are bound

- "Adding a variant to `InterfoldEventData` is APPEND-ONLY" — the r2 proof bundle event.

### 4.4 C7 is currently wrong-sized for CKKS (and it matters for the fold)

`decrypted_shares_aggregation_ckks` inherits `MAX_MSG_NON_ZERO_COEFFS = 100` from BFV,
where the plaintext is sparse. A CKKS `u_global` is dense over all N coefficients, and the

C7's `expected_d_commitments` check on 100 coefficients **cannot match the C6 output** at any

`max_k = N`). Consequence: a *fully-proven* decryption needs C7 widened to N (§7 item 4),
which puts C7 at ⚠ ≈ 7.5 M constraints at S1 (t + 1 = 3: Lagrange + CRT glue over
3 × 18 × 32768 coefficients ≈ 76 constraints per coefficient-limb) — no longer free. The
Track B alternative is to fold C7 over coefficient chunks like C6.

## 5. Folding plan (Track B design — no implementation)

### 5.1 Kernel granularity and step counts

Rule: a kernel step = one leaf circuit over a chunk of `c` consecutive coefficients of every
polynomial witness, sized so the leaf ≤ ~4 M constraints (browser bb.js ceiling and a
≈ 22 GiB prove on a 36 GiB box). The leaf keeps *all* limbs (L or LT) so the Fiat–Shamir
challenge `gamma` and every commitment can be computed per chunk; the accumulator chains
chunk commitments and a running evaluation.

**But**: the Greco/C1/C6/C8 identity is checked *at gamma* over the full polynomial
(`Σ_i coeff_i · γ^i`), and the `r1 · q + r2 · cyclo` quotients are full-length. Chunking a
polynomial identity at a single evaluation point needs the chunks to share `gamma`, which
is derived from a transcript over ALL witnesses. Two ways, both used in the literature:

- **(K1) Two-pass Horner fold** (recommended): step 0 computes the commitment of every
  witness chunk-by-chunk (Poseidon over chunks — commitments are already chunk-hashable
  since `flatten` packs coefficients in order), the accumulator ends with the full
  transcript hash → `gamma`; a second pass of steps evaluates each chunk's contribution
  `Σ_{i∈chunk} coeff_i · γ^{i}` with `γ^{chunk·c}` carried in the accumulator. Range checks
  live in the pass-1 kernels. The final step asserts the identity. Cost ≈ 2 passes ×
  the leaf work; recursive-verifier overhead ≈ 0.9 M constraints per step.
- **(K2) Per-limb split**: keep all N coefficients but one limb per step. Simpler (each
  step is today's circuit with L = 1), but a single-limb leaf at S2 is still ≈ 33 · 65536
  ≈ 2.2 M (C6) — fine — and gamma can be per-limb (the current circuits derive one gamma
  over all limbs; a per-limb gamma with the limb index in the transcript is equally
  sound). **K2 is the pragmatic choice for the committee circuits**: leaf = existing
  circuit body at `L = 1`, `LT = 1`; steps = L (18 / 38) or LT (20 / 41).

Step counts under K2:

| circuit | leaf at S1 (L = 1, N = 32768) ⚠ | steps S1 | steps S2 | fold overhead ⚠ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | 0.85 M | 18 | 38 | 18 × 0.9 M |
| C2a / C2b | 0.55 M / 1.0 M | 18 | 38 | |
| C6 | 1.1 M | 18 | 38 | |
| C7 (widened) | 0.4 M | 18 | 38 | |
| C8 r1 digit | 1.34 M | 20 | 41 | per digit |
| C8 r2 digit | 1.0 M | 20 | 41 | per digit |
| Greco ct0 / ct1 | ⚠ 1.3 M | 18 | 38 | client |

Per-step prover cost ⚠: leaf (≈ 12–17 s / M) + recursive verifier (≈ 0.9 M → ≈ 10 s,
[M] BFV `c6_fold` step ≈ 5 s on M4 Pro at 754 k constraints). So a K2 fold of C6 at S1 is
18 × (13 + 10) ≈ 7 min single-threaded-per-step on a 36 GiB box — vs 5.6 min big-circuit on
a 128 GiB box. Same CPU order, memory 5× lower; the price is engineering (§7).

### 5.2 Committee aggregation (C5-style)

Mirror of `dkg_aggregator` / `decryption_aggregator`:

- **`ckks_node_fold`**: verifies C1 (ZK), `c2ab_fold` (non-ZK), `c8_fold` (non-ZK, new)
  for one party; asserts `c1_public[0] == c2a expected` , `c1_public[2] == c2b expected`,
  `c1_public[0] == c8_fold.s_commitment` (the C8 gate's sk anchor, in-circuit). Exports

- **`ckks_dkg_aggregator`**: `nodes_fold` + C5-CKKS (pk aggregation: joint pk =

### 5.3 C8 round 2 — the missing circuit (specification)

Source of truth: `fhe.rs/crates/fhe/src/trckks/hybrid_gen.rs::round_2` (lines 173–214).
With `h0[j], h1[j]` the ROUND-1 AGGREGATES (`CkksHybridRelinKeyShare<R1Aggregated>`, sum over
all parties), party `i` publishes for every digit `j`:

```
h0'_i[j] = h0[j] · s_i            + e'_{0,i,j}
h1'_i[j] = h1[j] · (u_i − s_i)    + e'_{1,i,j}
```

over `Q·P` (`CkksQpPoly`: the same integer polynomial reduced mod every `q_l`, `l < L`, and
every `p_m`). `s_i` = the party's DKG secret contribution (CBD(0.5), `|c| ≤ 1`), `u_i` = the
SAME ephemeral secret as round 1 (`new_from_seed`; `|c| ≤ 2·variance`), `e'` = fresh CBD
noise (`CkksQpPoly::small`, `|c| ≤ 2·variance`). Aggregate: `b_j = Σ_i h0'_i[j] + Σ_i
h1'_i[j] = −h1[j]·s + P·g_j·s² + e'`, `a_j = h1[j]`.

**Circuit `relin_round2_hybrid_ckks_digit<N, D, LT, BIT_*>`** (per digit `j`, mirrors the
r1 digit circuit; one leg per row `l ∈ [0, LT)`):

Public inputs:
- `s_commitment` — MUST equal the party's C1-CKKS `sk_commitment` and its r1 `s_commitment`
  (`compute_share_computation_sk_commitment::<N, BIT_SK>(s)`).
- `u_commitment` — MUST equal the r1 `u_commitment` (same `u_i` in both rounds is a
  *protocol* requirement — `hybrid_gen.rs` line 22 — and this is the only place it becomes
  verifiable).
- `digit` (`< D`).
- `h0_agg_commitment`, `h1_agg_commitment` — `compute_ciphertext_commitment::<N, LT, BIT_H>`
  of the aggregated round-1 rows of digit `j` (public witness: every verifier recomputes it
  from the bytes it aggregated itself; the r1 aggregate is deterministic given the verified r1
  shares).

Private witnesses: `s, u: Polynomial<N>`; `e0', e1': Polynomial<N>`; `h0_agg[l], h1_agg[l]:
[Polynomial<N>; LT]` (limb rows of the aggregate, in `[0, q_l)`); `h0'[l], h1'[l]` (this
party's r2 rows); quotients `r1_h0'[l]: Polynomial<2N−1>`, `r2_h0'[l]: Polynomial<N−1>`
and the `h1'` twins.

Constraints, at the FS challenge `gamma = compute_threshold_pk_challenge(payload)` with
`payload = digit ‖ flatten(h0_agg) ‖ flatten(h1_agg) ‖ flatten(e0') ‖ flatten(e1') ‖
s_commitment ‖ u_commitment ‖ h0_agg_commitment ‖ h1_agg_commitment ‖ r2_share_commitment ‖
flatten(r1/r2 quotients)`:

```
for l in 0..LT:
  h0'[l](γ) == h0_agg[l](γ)·s(γ)            + e0'(γ) + r1_h0'[l](γ)·q_l + r2_h0'[l](γ)·(γ^N + 1)
  h1'[l](γ) == h1_agg[l](γ)·(u(γ) − s(γ))   + e1'(γ) + r1_h1'[l](γ)·q_l + r2_h1'[l](γ)·(γ^N + 1)
```
where `q_l` runs over `[q_0..q_{L−1}, p_0..p_{k−1}]` (the r1 `Configs<D, LT>::qis`; no gadget
term — round 2 has none, which is why r2 is ≈ 25 % smaller than r1 per row). Range checks:
`s ∈ [−1, 1]`, `u, e0', e1' ∈ [−2·var, 2·var]`, `h0_agg, h1_agg, h0', h1' ∈ [0, q_l)`,
`r2 ∈ [−(q_l−1)/2, (q_l−1)/2]`, `r1` bound `((N·q_bound·(u_bound + sk_bound) + 2·q_bound +
e_bound) / q_l)` — the r1 builder's envelope (`relin_round1_hybrid_ckks.rs` lines 209–231)
with the product bound `N · q_bound · max(|s|, |u − s|)` replacing `N · u_bound · q_bound`
since the multiplier is now the *aggregate* row (full-range, `q_bound`) times a small
secret. Output: `r2_share_commitment = compute_ciphertext_commitment::<N, LT, BIT_H>(h0', h1')`.

Bindings enforced OUTSIDE the circuit (node gate, extension of `check_relin_round_1_bindings`):
`digit == j`; `s_commitment` == C1 anchor == r1 `s_commitment`; `u_commitment` == r1
`u_commitment` (uniform across the `2·dnum` proofs); `h0_agg_commitment / h1_agg_commitment ==
digit_share_commitments_from_bytes(r1 aggregate)[j]` (new helper, same split as the r1
one); `r2_share_commitment == digit_share_commitments_from_bytes(received r2 share)[j]`.
R2 aggregation waits for all `n · dnum` r2 proofs (INVARIANTS "C8 gate" extended).

Witness builder: `zk-helpers/…/relin_round2_hybrid_ckks_digit.rs` mirroring
`compute_digit_inputs` (r1). fhe.rs needs `CkksHybridRelinKeyGenerator::round_2_extended`
returning `(share, e0', e1')` and `u_poly()` — the same gap already noted for round 1
(`round_1_extended`) in the prod-decision doc; today the interfold builder recomputes the
share from explicit `u/e0/e1` via `compute_round_1_share`, and the r2 builder can do the
same with a `compute_round_2_share(params, r1_agg, s, u, e0', e1')`.

Size ⚠: per (coeff × limb) ≈ 31 constraints (r1's 41 minus the gadget-scalar term and one
range-checked witness per leg) → S1 digit ≈ 20 M, S2 ≈ 82 M (§2.4).

**Aggregation of digit proofs into one per-ceremony proof** (`c8_fold`, Track B): per party
a sequential fold over `2 · dnum` leaves (r1 digits 0..dnum−1 then r2 digits), accumulator
slots `(s_c, u_c, share_c[dnum], r2_share_c[dnum], h0_agg_c[dnum], h1_agg_c[dnum])`; step
`t` asserts `digit == t mod dnum`, `s_c/u_c` equal to slot 0's (uniform), and for r2 steps
that `h0_agg_c[j] / h1_agg_c[j]` equal the values the *aggregator* supplies as public inputs
(bound on-chain to the published r1 aggregate). Then `ckks_node_fold` takes `c8_fold`'s
public tail. Committee-level: `nodes_fold` × H → `ckks_dkg_aggregator` checks that every
party's `h0_agg_c[j]` is the same value (one aggregate per ceremony). Proof count on-chain:
one.

### 5.4 Ceremony kernel split for S2 (Track B for C8)

K2 (one limb-row of `Q·P` per step) turns each digit proof into `LT` steps of
`≈ 41 · N` (r1) / `31 · N` (r2) constraints = 2.7 M / 2.0 M at S2 — provable in ≈ 15 GiB.
Fold chain per party per digit per round; `2 · dnum` chains → one `c8_fold`. The transcript
must include the limb index `l` so that a per-limb `gamma` is sound (the r1 identity is
already per-limb; only the shared `gamma` couples limbs, and that coupling is not needed for
soundness — each limb's identity holds independently mod `q_l`).

## 7. Ordered implementation list (effort in engineer-days; "M" = blocks multiplications)

To reach **fully proven at S1**:

1. **C7 widening to N coefficients** (`MAX_MSG_NON_ZERO_COEFFS` → N for the CKKS package,
   `u_global_commitment` output, witness builder, aggregator cross-check) — 2 d. Blocks
   *any* proven CKKS decryption at N > 100, not only multiplications.
2. **C8 round-2 digit circuit** (§5.3) + witness builder + fhe.rs
   `round_2_extended`/`compute_round_2_share` + `check_relin_round_2_bindings` + gate
   extension + `RelinCeremonyProofSigned` round-2 bundle (APPEND-ONLY event) — **5 d. [M]**
   Without it the ceremony is half-proven: a malicious r2 share yields a wrong key (denial)
   or, worse, a key with a known trapdoor term.
3. **Secure-preset codegen for every committee circuit** (`ckks_preset_for_param_set` for
   S1/S2 ids, `_ps<N>` packages for C1/C6/C7/C8r1/C8r2/C2a/C2b at S1) — 2 d. Compile on a
   rented ≥ 256 GiB box (S1) — 1 d of machine time; artifact staging (`stage-ckks-circuits.sh`)
   — 1 d. **[M]** (C8 shapes only exist at N = 512).
4. **Node prover memory model**: today every proof is proven in-process by the ciphernode's
   multithread pool; at S1 a C8 digit needs 150 GiB. Either (a) per-party prover host with
   ≥ 256 GiB and `BENCHMARK_MULTITHREAD_JOBS = 1`, or (b) an out-of-process prover service
   the node hands *witness files* to (secrets stay on the party's own box). — 3 d for (b).
5. **CKKS `node_fold` / `c8_fold` / `ckks_dkg_aggregator` / `ckks_decryption_aggregator`**
   (Track B aggregators, §5.2; C5-CKKS pk aggregation included) + zk-prover orchestration +
   EVM verifier for the two aggregator VKs — **12–15 d.** Needed for one-proof-per-stage
   on-chain verification; without it the contract would verify `n × (3 + 2·dnum)` leaves.
6. **C3/C4 at the DKG transport preset for CKKS** (share encryption / decryption proofs the
   BFV DKG has and the CKKS shell publishes empty) — 4 d if the secure transport preset
   exists (BFV secure-8192 circuits are 3.5 M / 1.7 M constraints [M]); a *secure* transport
   preset whose `t_dkg` ≥ every 40/60-bit CKKS limb is itself an open fhe-params item.
7. **Timing/ops**: `ckks_timing` marks around every proof + a `--proofs-only` bench mode so
   the S1 numbers in §6.2 can be replaced by [M] rows — 1 d.

S1 fully-proven total ≈ **30–35 engineer-days** + one 256 GiB machine per party (or 512 GiB
for parallel digits). Items 2, 3 are the ones that specifically block *multiplications*
(everything else is needed for an additions-only pipeline too).

To reach **fully proven at S2** (after S1):

8. **K2 kernel split** (one limb-row per step) for C1/C2a/C2b/C6/C7/C8r1/C8r2 + per-limb
   `gamma` transcript change in every circuit (a *soundness-reviewed* change: the limb
   index must enter the transcript) + `*_fold_kernel` genesis circuits + zk-prover step
   orchestration — **15–20 d.** Or: no split, 1 TiB boxes per party (§3.2) — 0 d, ≈ 9 h CPU
   per party per E3 (§6.2).
9. **Greco K2 client fold** (ct0/ct1 legs in bb.js, ≤ 4 M constraints per step, fold in a
   worker; or server-assisted with the client keeping `u`, `e0`, `e1` — a different trust
   posture) — 10 d. Blocks *any* user input at secure N, additions or multiplications.
10. Reduce ceremony proof volume: `alpha`/`dnum` trade (fewer, larger digits) or a
    *batched* r1/r2 digit circuit proving 2–3 digits per proof under one gamma — 3 d, saves
    ≈ 30 % fold overhead, not leaf CPU.

## 8. Verdict

A fully-proven, multiplying threshold-CKKS E3 at **S1** is reachable with the existing circuit
shapes and ≈ 30–35 engineer-days (the C8 round-2 circuit and the C7 widening are the two
hard blockers; the aggregators are the biggest chunk of work): per party ≈ **1.5 h of
prover CPU per E3 for DKG + ceremony** (of which the ceremony's 18 digit proofs are ≈ 75 min)
on a ≥ 256 GiB box, then ≈ 6 min per threshold decryption, all ⚠ extrapolated from a
linear-in-`N·L` constraint curve measured to N = 8192 (§2, R² > 0.9999) and bb's measured
12–17 s / 5.5 GiB per 10⁶ constraints. Folding at S1 is only needed for the per-committee
aggregators (one proof per stage on-chain), not for prover feasibility. At **S2** every
per-party leaf exceeds 350 GiB of prove RAM and the ceremony alone is ≈ 7–13 h of CPU per
party per E3: S2 needs either 1 TiB provers or the K2 limb-row fold (≈ 15–20 more days) and
should be treated as a research-grade target until S1 is proven live. Today (2026-09-04)
**nothing above N = 512 is proven end-to-end**: the demo-shape budget in §6.1 (≈ 150 s of
proving per party per E3, ceremony-dominated) is what the user's live run should reproduce.
