# E3 Lifecycle

The Interfold contract (`Interfold.sol`) moves each E3 through a fixed set of stages. This page
gives the call that enters each stage, who makes the call, the events, and what happens next. It
also covers the deadlines, the failure reasons, and refunds. It is for developers who request or
watch E3s and for operators who serve on committees.

The stages are the values of `E3Stage` in `interfaces/IInterfold.sol`: `None`, `Requested`,
`CommitteeFinalized`, `KeyPublished`, `CiphertextReady`, `Complete`, and `Failed`. `None` means that
the E3 does not exist. Every stage change emits `E3StageChanged(e3Id, previousStage, newStage)`.

<Lifecycle label='E3 lifecycle'>
  <Stage title='Requested' actor='Requester' event='E3Requested'>

The requester calls `Interfold.request(params)` and pays the fee. The contract stores the E3 and
asks the `CiphernodeRegistry` for a committee. Next, ciphernodes submit tickets after the VRF result
arrives.

  </Stage>
  <Stage title='CommitteeFinalized' actor='Ciphernodes' event='CommitteeFinalized'>

After the ticket window closes, a ciphernode calls `CiphernodeRegistry.finalizeCommittee(e3Id)`. The
registry locks the committee and calls the Interfold contract. Next, the committee runs DKG.

  </Stage>
  <Stage title='KeyPublished' actor='Aggregator' event='CommitteeFormed'>

The active aggregator calls `CiphernodeRegistry.publishCommittee` with the DKG proof. The registry
verifies the proof and calls the Interfold contract. Next, data providers encrypt and submit inputs.

  </Stage>
  <Stage title='CiphertextReady' actor='Application server' event='CiphertextOutputReferencePublished'>

After the input window closes, the compute output goes to `Interfold.publishCiphertextOutput`. The
contract checks the availability receipt and the compute proof. Next, the committee computes
decryption shares.

  </Stage>
  <Stage title='Complete' actor='Aggregator' event='PlaintextOutputPublished'>

The active aggregator calls `Interfold.publishPlaintextOutput` with the decryption proof. The
contract verifies the proof, stores the plaintext, and credits rewards. The E3 ends.

  </Stage>
  <Stage title='Failed' actor='Anyone' event='E3Failed' tone='fail'>

A deadline passes, committee formation fails, or the committee loses too many members. After a
deadline, anyone can call `Interfold.markE3Failed(e3Id)`. Next, anyone calls
`processE3Failure(e3Id)` to start refunds.

  </Stage>
</Lifecycle>

## Stage reference

| Stage                | Call that enters it                                                         | Usual caller             | Interfold events                                       |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| `Requested`          | `Interfold.request`                                                         | Requester                | `E3Requested`, `E3StageChanged`                        |
| `CommitteeFinalized` | `CiphernodeRegistry.finalizeCommittee`, then `onCommitteeFinalized`         | A committee candidate    | `CommitteeFinalized`, `E3StageChanged`                 |
| `KeyPublished`       | `CiphernodeRegistry.publishCommittee`, then `onCommitteePublished`          | Active aggregator        | `CommitteeFormed`, `E3StageChanged`                    |
| `CiphertextReady`    | `Interfold.publishCiphertextOutput`                                         | Application server       | `CiphertextOutputReferencePublished`, `E3StageChanged` |
| `Complete`           | `Interfold.publishPlaintextOutput`                                          | Active aggregator        | `PlaintextOutputPublished`, `E3StageChanged`           |
| `Failed`             | `markE3Failed`, `cancelE3`, or a call from the registry or slashing manager | Anyone, after a deadline | `E3Failed`, `E3StageChanged`                           |

`finalizeCommittee`, `publishCommittee`, `publishCiphertextOutput`, and `publishPlaintextOutput`
have no caller restriction. Stage checks, deadline checks, and proof verification protect them. The
"Usual caller" column shows who sends the transaction in the current software.

## Requested

The requester calls `request(E3RequestParams)`. The parameters are `committeeSize`, `inputWindow`,
`e3Program`, `paramSet`, `computeProviderParams`, `customParams`, `expectedFeeToken`,
`expectedCryptoConfigId`, and `maxFee`. The contract rejects the request in these conditions:

- Requests are paused, or the E3 program is not registered.
- The fee token is not allowed, or it is different from `expectedFeeToken`.
- The circuit configuration for `paramSet` is different from `expectedCryptoConfigId`.
- The quote from `getE3Quote` is more than `maxFee`.
- `inputWindow[0]` is before the current time, or `inputWindow[1]` is before `inputWindow[0]`.
- The worst-case lifecycle duration is more than `maxDuration`.

When the checks pass, the contract takes the fee. It keeps the service fee in escrow and credits the
flat randomness fee to the treasury. It calls the program `validate` function, which returns the
encryption scheme. It also records the timeout configuration and the dependent contracts for this
E3.

The contract then calls `CiphernodeRegistry.requestCommittee`. The registry checks that at least N
operators were active just before the request. It records the ticket price and the IMT root, and it
requests one random word from the randomness provider. The registry emits
`CommitteeRandomnessRequested` and `DkgFoldAttestationContextEstablished`.

After the Chainlink VRF v2.5 result arrives, ciphernodes call `submitTicket(e3Id, ticketNumber)`.
Each accepted ticket emits `TicketSubmitted`. The ticket window is `sortitionSubmissionWindow`
seconds from the VRF fulfillment time. See [Sortition](/internals/sortition).

## CommitteeFinalized

After the committee deadline, anyone can call `finalizeCommittee(e3Id)`. In the current software,
committee candidates schedule this call 30 seconds apart by party index, so one member acts first.

The registry then checks the result of sortition:

- If no usable VRF result exists, the E3 fails with `CommitteeFormationTimeout`.
- If fewer than N tickets were submitted, the E3 fails with `InsufficientCommitteeMembers`.
- Otherwise, the registry sorts the committee by address and calls `onCommitteeFinalized`.

In both failure cases, the registry also emits `CommitteeFormationFailed`.

`onCommitteeFinalized` sets the DKG deadline and records the reward recipient for each member. The
Interfold contract emits `CommitteeFinalized`. The registry emits
`SortitionCommitteeFinalized(e3Id, committee, scores)` and `CommitteeActivationChanged(e3Id, true)`.

Next, the committee runs DKG off-chain. The members exchange encrypted key shares and proofs (C0 to
C4). The active aggregator selects a roster of H ready members and proves the key aggregation (C5).
See [Cryptography](/learn/cryptography).

## KeyPublished

The active aggregator calls `publishCommittee(e3Id, pkCommitment, proof, dkgAttestationBundle)`. The
registry verifies the DKG proof with `BfvPkVerifier` and the fold attestations with
`DkgFoldAttestationVerifier`. It stores the DKG anchors that the decryption proof uses later.

The registry then calls `onCommitteePublished`. The Interfold contract accepts the key only before
the DKG deadline and before `inputWindow[1]`. It sets the compute deadline and emits
`CommitteeFormed`. The registry emits `CommitteeProofPublished`.

The committee members also publish the serialized public key in chunks with
`publishCommitteePublicKey`. Each chunk emits `CommitteePublicKeyChunkPublished`. A client rebuilds
the key and checks it against `pkCommitment` before it encrypts.

Next, data providers encrypt their inputs and call `publishInput` on the E3 program. The E3 program
decides which inputs to accept during the input window.

## CiphertextReady

The compute provider runs the secure process after `inputWindow[1]`. An application server then
calls `publishCiphertextOutput(e3Id, encodedOutputReference)`. The reference contains the
`contentHash` of the ciphertext bytes, the `ciphertextCommitment`, the `computeProof`, and the
`availabilityProof`.

The contract accepts the output only in these conditions:

- The E3 is in `KeyPublished`, and the compute deadline has not passed.
- The input window has closed.
- The committee still has enough active members.
- The E3 program accepts the availability receipt for `contentHash`.
- The ciphertext verifier and the E3 program `verify` function accept the compute proof.

The contract stores the hash and the commitment, and it sets the decryption deadline. It emits
`CiphertextOutputReferencePublished`. Next, each committee member computes a decryption share with a
C6 proof.

## Complete

The active aggregator combines T + 1 decryption shares and proves the result (C7). It calls
`publishPlaintextOutput(e3Id, plaintextOutput, proof)`. The contract accepts the output only before
the decryption deadline, with a viable committee and a valid decryption proof.

The contract stores the plaintext and pays for the work:

1. It credits the protocol share (`protocolShareBps`, recorded at request time) to the treasury.
2. It splits the rest of the service fee among the active committee members.
3. Each reward recipient claims with `claimReward(e3Id)` or `claimRewards(e3Ids)`.

The contract emits `PlaintextOutputPublished`, `RewardsDistributed`, and `RewardCredited` for each
member. Applications read the result from `getE3(e3Id).plaintextOutput` or from the
`PlaintextOutputPublished` event.

## Failed

An E3 enters `Failed` in one of these ways:

- **Timeout.** After a deadline, anyone calls `markE3Failed(e3Id)`. The contract selects the reason
  from the current stage.
- **Committee formation.** `finalizeCommittee` records `CommitteeFormationTimeout` or
  `InsufficientCommitteeMembers`.
- **Committee viability.** A slash expels a member, and the active members drop below H. The
  `SlashingManager` records `InsufficientCommitteeMembers`.
- **Cancellation.** The requester calls `cancelE3(e3Id)`. This is possible only in `Requested`, when
  no usable VRF result exists and the committee deadline has passed. The reason is
  `CommitteeFormationTimeout`.

The contract emits `E3Failed(e3Id, failedAtStage, reason)`. If a later expulsion shows that the
committee caused a requester-side failure, the `SlashingManager` changes the reason to
`InsufficientCommitteeMembers`. The contract then emits `E3FailureReclassified`. This change is
possible only before refund settlement.

## Deadlines and timeouts

Each E3 records the timeout configuration at request time. A later governance change does not affect
an existing E3.

| Deadline            | Set when                 | Value                                                                                                                | Reason when it passes       |
| ------------------- | ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Committee deadline  | Request, then VRF result | Request time + `randomnessRequestTimeout`. After a usable VRF result: fulfillment time + `sortitionSubmissionWindow` | `CommitteeFormationTimeout` |
| DKG deadline        | `CommitteeFinalized`     | Committee deadline + `dkgWindow`                                                                                     | `DKGTimeout`                |
| Compute deadline    | `KeyPublished`           | The later of key publication time and `inputWindow[1]`, + `computeWindow`                                            | `ComputeTimeout`            |
| Decryption deadline | `CiphertextReady`        | Ciphertext publication time + `decryptionWindow`                                                                     | `DecryptionTimeout`         |

A late finalization does not move the DKG deadline. It leaves less time for DKG. In `Requested`, a
committee with enough tickets can still finalize until the DKG deadline. For that case,
`markE3Failed` accepts the failure only after the committee deadline + `dkgWindow`.

The committee key must arrive before `inputWindow[1]`. The contract rejects a later key, and the E3
then fails with `DKGTimeout`. A safe `inputWindow[1]` is later than the request time plus the
randomness timeout, the ticket window, and the DKG window.

The contract also limits the total duration. At request time, it computes the later of
`inputWindow[1]` and the committee setup time (`randomnessRequestTimeout` +
`sortitionSubmissionWindow` + `dkgWindow`). It adds `computeWindow` and `decryptionWindow`. The
result must not be more than `maxDuration`.

`markFailedGracePeriod` protects against short chain or RPC delays. During this period after a
deadline, only the requester, the contract owner, or an active committee member can call
`markE3Failed`. After the period, anyone can call it. A value of `0` removes the restriction.

### Configured values

The mainnet values come from
`packages/interfold-contracts/deploy/protocol/mainnet-protocol.config.json`. The local values are
the defaults for local development deployments (`scripts/deployInterfold.ts` and
`CiphernodeRegistryOwnable.sol`). Read the live values from the contracts: `getTimeoutConfig`,
`markFailedGracePeriod`, `maxDuration`, `sortitionSubmissionWindow`, and `randomnessRequestTimeout`.

| Parameter                   | Contract           | Mainnet               | Local development     |
| --------------------------- | ------------------ | --------------------- | --------------------- |
| `randomnessRequestTimeout`  | CiphernodeRegistry | 3,600 s (1 hour)      | 3,600 s (1 hour)      |
| `sortitionSubmissionWindow` | CiphernodeRegistry | 600 s (10 minutes)    | 60 s                  |
| `dkgWindow`                 | Interfold          | 21,600 s (6 hours)    | 7,200 s (2 hours)     |
| `computeWindow`             | Interfold          | 604,800 s (7 days)    | 86,400 s (1 day)      |
| `decryptionWindow`          | Interfold          | 21,600 s (6 hours)    | 3,600 s (1 hour)      |
| `markFailedGracePeriod`     | Interfold          | 3,600 s (1 hour)      | 0                     |
| `maxDuration`               | Interfold          | 2,592,000 s (30 days) | 2,592,000 s (30 days) |

## Failure reasons

`FailureReason` in `interfaces/IInterfold.sol` lists the reasons. `FailurePayerLib.sol` assigns the
cost of each reason to the requester side or to the ciphernode side.

| Value | Reason                         | Recorded by                                                    | Cost falls on |
| ----: | ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
|     0 | `None`                         | Not a failure                                                  | None          |
|     1 | `CommitteeFormationTimeout`    | `markE3Failed` in `Requested`, `finalizeCommittee`, `cancelE3` | Ciphernodes   |
|     2 | `InsufficientCommitteeMembers` | `finalizeCommittee`, `SlashingManager`                         | Ciphernodes   |
|     3 | `DKGTimeout`                   | `markE3Failed` in `CommitteeFinalized`                         | Ciphernodes   |
|     4 | `DKGInvalidShares`             | No contract path in this release                               | Ciphernodes   |
|     5 | `NoInputsReceived`             | No contract path in this release                               | Requester     |
|     6 | `ComputeTimeout`               | `markE3Failed` in `KeyPublished`                               | Requester     |
|     7 | `ComputeProviderExpired`       | No contract path in this release                               | Requester     |
|     8 | `ComputeProviderFailed`        | No contract path in this release                               | Requester     |
|     9 | `RequesterCancelled`           | Reserved for ABI compatibility. `cancelE3` records reason 1.   | Requester     |
|    10 | `DecryptionTimeout`            | `markE3Failed` in `CiphertextReady`                            | Ciphernodes   |
|    11 | `DecryptionInvalidShares`      | No contract path in this release                               | Ciphernodes   |
|    12 | `VerificationFailed`           | No contract path in this release                               | Ciphernodes   |

`onE3Failed` rejects `None`, `RequesterCancelled`, and any value from `_MAX_FAILURE_REASON` up.
Requester-side reasons cover failures of the requester, the data providers, and the compute
provider.

## Refunds

Interfold keeps only the service fee in escrow. The flat randomness fee goes to the treasury at
request time. No success, failure, or cancellation path returns the randomness fee.

After an E3 fails, the funds move in this sequence:

1. Anyone calls `processE3Failure(e3Id)`. The Interfold contract sends the service fee escrow to
   `E3RefundManager` and emits `E3FailureProcessed`.
2. `E3RefundManager` calculates the split from the failure reason and the policy recorded at request
   time.
3. The requester calls `claimRequesterRefund(e3Id)`.
4. The reward recipient of each honest committee member calls
   `claimHonestNodeReward(e3Id, operator)`.

The split depends on the side that caused the failure:

- **Ciphernode side.** The requester gets 100% of the service fee escrow. The treasury gets nothing,
  and the fee does not pay honest nodes. Honest nodes get compensation only from collateral slashed
  from faulty nodes.
- **Requester side.** Honest committee members get a share for the work that they completed. The
  treasury gets the protocol share. The requester gets the rest. If no honest node exists, the node
  share also goes to the requester.

The [Requesting an E3](/build/requesting-an-e3) page explains fees, quotes, and refund claims in
detail.
