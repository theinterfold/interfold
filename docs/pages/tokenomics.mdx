FOLD is the token of the Interfold. This page gives the total supply of FOLD, the allocation of that
supply across categories, and the unlock schedule of each category. It is for token holders,
operators, and anyone who wants to understand the distribution of FOLD. For the role of FOLD in the
network, see the [FOLD Token FAQ](/faq/fold-token).

## Token distribution

The total supply of FOLD is 1.2 billion.

<AllocationPie />

The allocations are in two groups: **Community** and **Other**. The sections below describe each
allocation.

## Community allocations

### Foundation Treasury

The Interfold has been built in the open. It is freely available as open-source software under the
LGPLv3 license. A portion of supply is allocated to The Interfold Foundation. Grants may be awarded
proactively to support priority work. Grants may also be awarded retroactively to recognize
meaningful contributions.

### CCA Sale

This allocation was reserved for two Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auctions (CCAs). The auctions gave
community members and early believers in The Interfold a fair opportunity to participate before
token transferability took place. For the auction details, see [The FOLD Auction](/faq/auction).

### Airdrop

3.94% of supply was airdropped to web3, privacy, and security experts and organizations. The airdrop
recognizes their contributions to the space. This allocation has already been executed and fully
allocated, and it vests over 24 months.

## Other allocations

### Gnosis Guild

This allocation recognizes the early role of Gnosis Guild. It is subject to a 48-month release
schedule.

### Investors

The Interfold, previously developed under the name Enclave, has completed these fundraising rounds:

| Round        | Valuation |
| ------------ | --------- |
| Pre-seed     | $20m      |
| Seed         | $25.8m    |
| Legion round | $25.8m    |

This allocation combines all three rounds. It has no lock policy, and it becomes transferable in
full at the TGE (token generation event). The Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auctions are shown
separately, under the CCA Sale allocation.

### Team and Advisors

This allocation covers the core team and the advisors who supported the early development of the
Interfold. It follows a 24-month release schedule.

## Token contract rules

The FOLD token contract (`InterfoldToken`) applies these rules to all FOLD:

| Rule             | What the contract does                                                                                                 |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Name and symbol  | `Interfold` and `FOLD`. An ERC-20 token with the ERC-20 Permit and ERC-20 Votes extensions.                            |
| Supply cap       | `MAX_SUPPLY` is 1,200,000,000 FOLD. A mint above the cap reverts with `MaxSupplyExceeded`.                             |
| Minting          | Only the admin and minter roles can mint, and only before the TGE. After the TGE, a mint reverts with `MintingClosed`. |
| TGE              | Anyone can call `tge()` after the cooldown that follows the auction window. The call succeeds one time only.           |
| Allocation locks | An allocation can have a lock policy with an unlock curve. A wallet can transfer only its unlocked balance.            |
| Bonded FOLD      | Bonded FOLD counts toward the amount that a wallet with locked FOLD must keep. Locked FOLD can be bonded.              |

For the TGE date and what changes after it, see [FOLD Transferability](/faq/transferability). For
the address of the FOLD contract, see [Contract addresses](/reference/contracts).
