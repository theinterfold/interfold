# FOLD Transferability

General FOLD transferability started on Aug 19, 2026, as part of the Interfold launch sequence.

<Callout type='info'>
  **Status.** General FOLD transferability is active. The FOLD contract recorded the TGE on Aug 19,
  2026 at 14:00:23 UTC. `tgeTimestamp()` on the FOLD contract returns `1787148023`.
</Callout>

This guide explains when transferability can be enabled and what changes when it is active. It also
tells auction participants how to claim FOLD, and where to find official Interfold resources.

<Callout type='warning'>
  Use only official Interfold links and verified contract addresses. Do not trust unsolicited DMs,
  copied contract addresses, impersonator accounts, or unofficial claiming and trading links.
</Callout>

## Timing

### When does general FOLD transferability begin?

General FOLD transferability can be enabled at or after:

**Aug 19 at 14:00 UTC (10 AM ET)**

**Unix timestamp:** `1787148000`

At or after that timestamp, anyone can call the `tge()` function on the FOLD contract to enable
general transferability.

The timestamp does not automatically enable transfers by itself. General transferability begins when
the `tge()` transaction has successfully executed on-chain.

The function is permissionless: anyone can call it when the timestamp has passed. It does not depend
on a specific Interfold-controlled account.

Official Interfold channels will confirm when general transferability has been enabled.

The FOLD contract (`InterfoldToken`) enforces these rules for `tge()`:

- The earliest time is the end of the CCA window (`CCA_END`) plus a 40-day cooldown
  (`TGE_COOLDOWN`).
- A call before the earliest time reverts with `TgeTooEarly`.
- The first successful call records the TGE time and emits `TgeTriggered`.
- A second call reverts with `AlreadyLive`.

### Is FOLD transferability the same as Network Alpha?

No. General FOLD transferability and Network Alpha are distinct milestones in the broader Interfold
launch sequence.

**General FOLD transferability** changes the transfer state of FOLD.

**Network Alpha** is the first mainnet operating phase of the Interfold network.

For more about FOLD and its role in the network, see:

- [FOLD Token](/faq/fold-token)
- [FOLD Tokenomics](/tokenomics)

## What changes for holders

### What changes once general transferability is enabled?

When general FOLD transferability is enabled:

- FOLD can be transferred between addresses without the launch-period general transfer restriction.
- Holders can interact with FOLD through supported on-chain interfaces.
- FOLD can be used for supported network functions, including ciphernode bonding where available.
- Allocation-specific vesting, lockups, or contractual restrictions continue to apply where
  relevant.

General FOLD transferability does not override restrictions that apply to a specific FOLD
allocation.

In the FOLD contract, the TGE removes the pre-TGE transfer restriction. The lock check stays. A
wallet can transfer only its unlocked balance until the lock sunset time (`NO_MORE_LOCKS`).

### Does general transferability remove vesting or lockups?

No. General transferability does not override vesting schedules, contractual restrictions, or other
conditions that apply to specific FOLD allocations.

See [FOLD Tokenomics](/tokenomics) for currently published allocation and unlock information.

### Do I need to do anything to FOLD I already hold?

General FOLD transferability does not itself require holders to claim through the Auction 2
interface.

The terms of a specific allocation continue to govern any vesting, lockup, or contractual
restrictions that apply to that allocation.

For currently published allocation and unlock information, see [FOLD Tokenomics](/tokenomics).

### I participated in the FOLD auction. When can I claim?

Claiming for FOLD Auction 2 is expected to open immediately after the auction closes on Aug 19 at
**13:06 UTC (9:06 AM ET)**.

You can then claim allocated FOLD and any unspent USDC through the verified auction interface.

Claiming and general FOLD transferability are separate actions. Auction 2 closes shortly before
general FOLD transferability can be enabled.

## Using FOLD

### Can FOLD be used for ciphernode bonding?

FOLD supports ciphernode bonding as part of the economic operation of the Interfold network.

Ciphernode operators bond FOLD to become eligible for network duties and economically accountable
for correct participation.

The FOLD contract lets FOLD move to and from the bonding registry before the TGE as well. FOLD that
is under an allocation lock can also be bonded, because bonded FOLD counts toward the amount that
the wallet must keep.

For current operator requirements, setup instructions, and bonding information, see:

- [Ciphernode Operators](/operate)
- [Running a Ciphernode](/operate/running)
- [Registration and Bonding](/operate/registration)

Current operator documentation is the source of truth for network participation requirements and
bonding mechanics.

## Verification and safety

### What is the verified FOLD contract?

The canonical FOLD token contract (`InterfoldToken`, FOLD) on Ethereum Mainnet is:

[View the FOLD contract on Etherscan](https://etherscan.io/address/0xE172e9B6cfBeeB5593bDcE3f077356FDb33af904)

### How do I stay safe around FOLD transferability?

Periods around token transferability can attract impersonators and fraudulent links.

Before you interact with FOLD:

- Start from an official Interfold website, documentation page, or verified social account.
- Verify the FOLD contract address before you sign a transaction.
- Do not trust contract addresses, claiming links, or trading links sent through unsolicited DMs.
- Do not send assets to anyone who offers manual claiming, migration, unlocking, or support.
- Never share your seed phrase, private key, or wallet backup.
- Review the connected network, destination, and transaction details before you sign.

Interfold team members and moderators will never ask for your seed phrase, private key, wallet
backup, or access to your wallet.
