# The FOLD Auction

This FAQ is about FOLD Auction 2, the second public FOLD auction. It is for people who want to take
part in the auction or claim FOLD after it. The questions are in four groups: the auction itself,
participation, claiming and transferability, and safety.

## About the auction

### What is FOLD Auction 2?

FOLD Auction 2 is the second public FOLD auction. It uses the Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction
mechanism.

It follows the initial FOLD Auction, which was held July 8–10, 2026.

Auction 2 is intentionally limited to **2% of total FOLD supply**. It is held around the transition
to general FOLD transferability. Its purpose is to broaden distribution and help bootstrap deeper,
longer-term on-chain liquidity.

### Why is there a second FOLD Auction?

Auction 2 is a limited distribution step before general FOLD transferability on Aug 19.

It is intentionally small at **2% of total FOLD supply**. It begins with a floor that matches the
clearing price of the first FOLD Auction.

The auction gives a transparent, public mechanism for additional distribution before FOLD becomes
generally transferable. It also supports long-term on-chain liquidity.

**50% of proceeds will be dedicated to long-term on-chain liquidity support.**

Auction 2 closes shortly before general FOLD transferability can be enabled.

### What happened in the first FOLD Auction?

The initial FOLD Auction ran from **July 8–10, 2026**. It used the Uniswap Continuous Clearing
Auction mechanism.

The first auction is complete. Its clearing price was **0.02154816 USDC / FOLD**, which is also the
floor for FOLD Auction 2.

Participants from the first auction can continue to access claiming and post-auction information
through the official [Interfold auction page](https://www.theinterfold.com/fold-auction).

### What is a Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction?

A Continuous Clearing Auction (CCA) is a price-discovery mechanism for token distribution. It is
built on Uniswap infrastructure.

- The auction runs across multiple discrete periods.
- Participants submit bids through the official Uniswap CCA interface.
- Successful bids within a period clear at the same clearing price.
- The minimum price for each subsequent period is the greater of the original floor price or the
  clearing price of the previous period.
- The minimum price may rise in later periods. It does not fall below the applicable prior clearing
  price or the original floor.
- Any tokens not distributed earlier remain available in the final period.

The CCA distributes tokens progressively and applies a uniform price within each period. This design
is intended to reduce gas competition, mitigate sniping, and let clearing prices develop through
auction participation.

[Read more about Uniswap CCAs](https://cca.uniswap.org).

### Does the auction determine the price of FOLD after transferability?

No. The auction determines allocations and a clearing price within the auction itself. It does not
determine the market price of FOLD when general transferability begins.

Future market prices cannot be predicted.

## Participation

### How do I participate?

1. Go to the official [FOLD Auction page](https://www.theinterfold.com/fold-auction).
2. Follow the verified link to the Uniswap CCA interface.
3. Complete the required verification process through the official flow.
4. Submit your bid during the auction window.
5. After the auction closes, claim any allocated FOLD and unspent USDC through the verified auction
   interface.

Before you participate:

- Make sure that you have USDC available on Ethereum Mainnet.
- Review the auction terms and eligibility requirements.
- Use only links published through official Interfold channels.
- Never share your seed phrase, private key, or wallet backup.

### Who can participate?

Participation is available to eligible participants. It is subject to jurisdiction, identity
verification, AML and sanctions screening, and other applicable restrictions.

The official verification process determines eligibility. The auction terms govern eligibility.

Completing verification does not guarantee eligibility or an allocation.

### Is KYC required?

Yes. Participants must complete identity verification through the official verification process
before they participate.

The verification process determines eligibility and conducts applicable AML and sanctions screening.

Participation remains subject to the auction terms and applicable restrictions.

## Claiming and transferability

### When can participants claim FOLD?

Claiming is expected to open immediately after the auction closes.

Participants can then claim allocated FOLD and any unspent USDC through the verified auction
interface.

Claiming and general FOLD transferability are separate actions.

### When does general FOLD transferability begin?

FOLD Auction 2 closes Aug 19 at **13:06 UTC (9:06 AM ET)**.

General FOLD transferability can be enabled shortly afterward, at or after **14:00 UTC (10 AM ET)**.

At or after Unix timestamp **`1787148000`**, anyone can call the `tge()` function on the FOLD
contract to enable general transferability.

When that transaction is executed, FOLD becomes generally transferable.

FOLD Auction 2, claiming, general FOLD transferability, and Network Alpha are distinct milestones
within the broader Interfold launch sequence. For details, see
[FOLD Transferability](/faq/transferability).

## Safety

### How do I know I’m using the official auction?

Use only auction, claiming, verification, and support links published through official Interfold
channels.

Official Interfold channels are:

- [Interfold auction page](https://www.theinterfold.com/fold-auction)
- [Interfold website](https://www.theinterfold.com/)
- [Interfold X account](https://x.com/theinterfold)
- [Interfold documentation](https://docs.theinterfold.com/)

Do not trust:

- Auction, claiming, or support links sent through unsolicited DMs.
- Copied token or contract addresses from unofficial sources.
- Impersonator accounts.
- Private wallet-connection links.
- Requests for your seed phrase, private key, or wallet backup.
- Requests for payment in exchange for support.

<Callout type='warning'>
  Interfold team members and moderators will never ask for your seed phrase, private key, wallet
  backup, or access to your wallet.
</Callout>
