# Operator Identity

This FAQ explains the identity check for the bond owners of ciphernodes. It is for operators and for
people who want to know how the network protects its committees. For the procedure, see
[Bond-Owner Identity](/operate/identity).

### Why does the Interfold check the identity of bond owners?

Each E3 uses a small committee. If enough members of one committee work together, they can decrypt
the inputs. A committee takes at most one operator from each bond owner, but one person can control
many addresses. The identity check connects each bond owner to one identity document. Thus it is
more difficult for one person or a small group to control a committee.

### Does every operator have to verify?

Governance decides. First it publishes the identity registry with the check off, and bond owners can
verify. Later it makes the check required. The hosted dashboard shows the current state in the
**Identity required** row of the **Bond-owner identity** panel.

### What happens if I do not verify?

Before the check is required, nothing changes. After that, the operator stays registered and active.
It keeps its bond and its tickets, and it completes the E3s of its current committees. It does not
get a seat in a new committee.

### What does the Interfold learn about me?

The ZKPassport app reads the document on your phone, and the data of the document stays on the
phone. The registration makes an identifier of the document, the bond-owner address, and a
valid-until date public on the chain. The dashboard sets this date to one year after today, so the
expiry date of your document stays private. If the document expires sooner, you enter its expiry
date or an earlier date. The identity registry keeps no name, nationality, birth date, document
number, or photo. Other services get a different identifier for the same document. The issuing state
has the full chip data, so it can calculate the identifier and connect it to you.

### Which documents can I use?

You can use a passport, national ID card, or residence permit with a chip. ZKPassport supports most
documents that comply with ICAO 9303. You also need a phone with NFC. Proofs from Aadhaar, the
Japanese My Number card, mobile driving licenses, or photos of the data page do not work.

### How often do I verify?

A credential lasts at most one year. Verify again each year, at least 30 days before the credential
ends. Use the same document while it is valid. If the document expires sooner, the credential ends
on its expiry date. Then verify with your new document.

### Can one person run several ciphernodes?

Yes. One bond owner can fund many operators. Each committee still takes at most one operator from
each bond owner.

### Can a Safe be the bond owner?

Yes. The dashboard makes a file for the Safe Transaction Builder. The Safe then sends the
registration.

### What if my document is lost or stolen?

Verify your new document. The identifier of the earlier document stays reserved for your bond owner,
so nobody else can register it. When FaceMatch is required, it is also difficult for another person
to make a proof with your document.
