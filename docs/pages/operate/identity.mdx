# Bond-Owner Identity

This page explains the identity check for bond owners and how to pass it. It is for the bond owner
of a ciphernode. At the end, the bond owner has a credential in the identity registry, and you know
when to renew it.

## Why the check exists

A committee takes at most one operator from each bond owner. This rule counts addresses, and one
person can control many addresses. See
[Committee limits for each bond owner](/operate/registration#committee-limits-for-each-bond-owner).

The identity check connects each bond owner to one identity document. One document can back only one
bond owner. Thus it is more difficult for one person or a small group to control a committee.

The check proves one document, not one person. A person with two valid documents can verify two bond
owners. The check also does not stop a person who lends a document to another person.

## When the check applies

Governance turns on the check in two steps:

1. Governance publishes the identity registry with the check off. The operator guide of the hosted
   dashboard then shows the **Bond-owner identity** panel. Bond owners can verify, but committee
   selection does not change.
2. Later, governance makes the check required. Then sortition counts an operator only when its bond
   owner had a current credential at the snapshot before the request.

The **Identity required** row of the panel shows `Not yet`, or the date from which the check is
required. When the network has no identity registry, the panel does not show.

When the check is required, the operator of an unverified bond owner stays registered and active. It
keeps its bond and its tickets, and it completes the E3s of its current committees. It does not get
a seat in a new committee. Ciphernode releases from v0.20.0 apply the check when they select
candidates.

## What the check shows

The ZKPassport app reads the chip of the document on the phone. The data of the document stays on
the phone. The app sends a proof.

The proof shows these facts:

- The document is valid at least until the date in **Document valid until**.
- The proof is bound to the bond-owner address and to the network.
- When FaceMatch is required, a live face scan agreed with the photo in the chip.

The registration makes these values public on the chain: the identifier of the document, the
bond-owner address, the date in **Document valid until**, and the date when the credential ends. The
identity registry keeps no name, nationality, birth date, document number, or photo.

A credential lasts at most one year, so the panel fills in the date one year after today. Thus the
public date does not show the expiry date of the document. You enter the expiry date only when the
document expires sooner. To keep that date private, enter an earlier date. The credential then ends
on the earlier date.

ZKPassport calculates the identifier from the chip data, and from the domain and the scope of the
Interfold verifier. Other services get a different identifier for the same document. A party that
has the full chip data, for example the issuing state, can calculate the identifier and connect it
to the person.

## What you need

- A passport, national ID card, or residence permit with a chip. ZKPassport supports most documents
  that comply with ICAO 9303. See the [ZKPassport FAQ](https://docs.zkpassport.id/faq).
- A phone with NFC and the [ZKPassport app](https://zkpassport.id).
- For FaceMatch, a phone that passes Google Play Integrity on Android or app attestation on Apple
  devices. On a phone that fails this check, the app does not make a FaceMatch proof.
- The bond-owner wallet in the browser. For a Safe, the Safe Transaction Builder.

The verifier accepts only proofs that read the chip of the document. It rejects Aadhaar, Japanese My
Number card, and mobile driving license proofs. It also rejects photos of the data page. Each of
these sources gives a person another identifier, so one person could verify a second bond owner.

## Verify the bond owner

<Steps>

### Open the identity panel

Open [Run a ciphernode](https://dashboard.theinterfold.com/#operator) in the hosted dashboard.

Connect a wallet.

In **Operator key (the ciphernode address)**, enter the operator address. The **Bond-owner
identity** panel shows below the setup steps when the operator has a bond owner.

### Check the date

**Document valid until (UTC date)** shows the date one year after today. The credential ends on this
date. A credential cannot last longer.

If the document expires before this date, enter the expiry date that is printed on the document, or
an earlier date. Use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`. With a later date, the app cannot make the proof.

### Create the request

Click **Create ZKPassport request**. The panel shows a QR code.

### Make the proof in the app

Scan the QR code with the ZKPassport app.

In the app, examine the site and the bound address. Then approve the request.

Scan the chip of the document.

If the app asks for a face scan, do the face scan.

The app makes the proof in up to one minute. Then the panel shows **Proof ready**, the bound
address, and the dates of the credential.

### Register the identity

For a bond owner with a browser wallet, connect the bond-owner wallet. Then click **Register the
identity**.

For a Safe, click **Download the Safe transaction**. Load the file in the Safe Transaction Builder,
and execute it before the time that the panel shows. The registration costs about 1.3 million gas.

Register before the date in **Register before**. After that date, the proof is too old, and you must
create a new request.

### Count the owner now

Do this step only when the check is required and the operator is registered. Each node refreshes its
own status when a new 30-day window starts. Until then, the operator does not count toward request
capacity.

To count the operator now, click **Count the owner now** after the registration. For a registered
operator, the Safe transaction already includes this refresh.

</Steps>

The **Credential** row of the panel then shows `Verified until` and the end date.

## Renew the credential

A credential lasts at most one year after the proof. It ends earlier when you entered an earlier
date. Thus a bond owner verifies again about once a year.

When the check is required, request capacity uses fixed 30-day windows. A bond owner counts in a
window only when its credential lasts until the end of that window. Renew at least 30 days before
the credential ends. If you renew later, the owner can stop counting before the credential ends. It
counts again after the renewal and the next status refresh.

Renew with the same document while it is valid. A renewal cannot end before the current credential.
Use the same procedure. For a verified owner, the panel title is **Renew the credential**.

## Replace a document

Verify the new document with the same procedure, for example after you get a new passport or after
you lose a document. The new document gets a new identifier. The identity registry accepts the new
credential when it does not end before the current credential.

If the new document ends before the current credential, the registry rejects it with
`IdentityExpiryReduced`. Then governance must revoke the current credential first.

The identifier of an earlier document stays reserved for this bond owner. Another bond owner cannot
register it, also after a replacement, an expiry, or a revocation. When FaceMatch is required, it is
difficult for another person to make a proof with a stolen document.

## Change the bond owner

The credential belongs to the bond-owner address, not to the operator. When the check is required
and the bond owner changes, the operator counts again only when the new owner has a credential. The
admission cooldown also starts again. See
[Change the bond owner](/operate/registration#change-the-bond-owner).

## Troubleshooting

| Message or error                                                                                             | Cause                                                                                                           | Action                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `This proof did not read the document's chip. Scan the chip of a passport or ID card in the ZKPassport app.` | The app used a source other than the chip, for example a photo of the data page.                                | Create a new request, and scan the chip.                                                   |
| `The proof is too old to register. Create a new request.`                                                    | The proof is older than the proof validity of the verifier, or the date in **Document valid until** has passed. | Create a new request with a date in the future.                                            |
| `This proof ends before the current credential. A renewal cannot shorten it.`                                | The new credential would end before the current credential.                                                     | Enter a later date. If the document ends earlier, ask governance to revoke the credential. |
| `This proof is bound to another owner or network. Create a new request.`                                     | The proof names a different address or network.                                                                 | Open the panel of the correct operator on the correct network. Then create a new request.  |
| `The request was rejected in the ZKPassport app.`                                                            | The request was declined in the app.                                                                            | Create a new request.                                                                      |
| `IdentityInUse`                                                                                              | Another bond owner registered this document first.                                                              | Verify with a different document.                                                          |
| `UnsupportedVerificationMethod`                                                                              | The proof did not read the chip of the document.                                                                | Create a new request, and scan the chip.                                                   |
| The app cannot make the proof                                                                                | The document expires before the date in **Document valid until**.                                               | Create a new request with the expiry date of the document, or an earlier date.             |
| The app does not find the chip                                                                               | The phone is not in contact with the chip, or the cover of the passport blocks it.                              | Keep the phone on the document and move it slowly. Open the passport at the photo page.    |

For more help with the app, see the [ZKPassport FAQ](https://docs.zkpassport.id/faq).

<Callout type='info'>
  When governance closes registration, for example during a ZKPassport version change, the panel
  shows that it cannot register now. Credentials that the registry already issued stay valid.
</Callout>
