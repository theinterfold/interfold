# Set up your operator

The node uses your selected execution client in DAppNode **Stakers**. Check that the client is
synced and its WebSocket RPC is enabled.

1. Open the package **Operator Dashboard** link. Follow the wallet steps to authorize your bond
   owner, bond FOLD, register, and buy tickets. Sepolia includes a test-token faucet.
2. Open the package **Node dashboard** link. Check that your operator is active and eligible for new
   committees.

For updates, leave the credential upload empty. Updates and restarts reuse your saved identity. Keep
an encrypted offline copy of the package **Backup**. Follow the operator exit procedure before
removing an active node.
