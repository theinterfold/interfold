# Public inputs of the negotiation proof

This file is the contract between the circuits, the Solidity program, the SDK and the client. Each
value is one BN254 field element. In Solidity it is a `bytes32` (big-endian, below the field
modulus). The array has no pairing-point elements: the generated verifier appends them itself.

## Offer proof: `negotiation_offer`

An offer is a tree of proofs. Only the top proof goes on chain. Solidity verifier:
`contracts/verifiers/NegotiationOfferVerifier.sol`, contract `NegotiationOfferVerifier`
(`verify(bytes proof, bytes32[] publicInputs) returns (bool)`). Eight public inputs, in this order:

| #   | Name            | Value                                                                               | Who sets it                     |
| --- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 0   | `ct_commitment` | The commitment of ciphertext 0 (lanes 0 to 7) (`commitments[0]` in the offer data). | the party, checked by the tree  |
| 1   | `ct_commitment` | The commitment of ciphertext 1 (lanes 8 to 15).                                     | the party, checked by the tree  |
| 2   | `ct_commitment` | The commitment of ciphertext 2 (the mask).                                          | the party, checked by the tree  |
| 3   | `party`         | `uint160(party)`: the sender, or the signer of a relayed offer.                     | the contract                    |
| 4   | `role`          | `0` for the seller, `1` for the buyer.                                              | the contract (from the address) |
| 5   | `levels`        | The number of price levels of the negotiation (2 to 16).                            | the contract (from storage)     |
| 6   | `pk_commitment` | The committee key commitment of the E3 (`e3.committeePublicKey`).                   | the contract                    |
| 7   | `vk_anchor`     | `vkAnchor` of the deployment (see below).                                           | the contract (immutable)        |

Inputs 0 to 5 are `pub` parameters of `main`. Inputs 6 and 7 are its return value.

The circuit proves:

- Each ciphertext of `ct_commitment[r]` is a well-formed encryption, under the committee key of
  `pk_commitment`, of the plaintext whose `k1` the circuit holds (Interfold user-data encryption
  proof). The three proofs share the encryption key row and the VK chains.
- `levels` is in 2 to 16, `role` is 0 or 1, and `party != 0`.
- The private `step` is below `levels`, and the private `share` is below 16.
- Ciphertexts 0 and 1 encrypt exactly the SIMD slots `laneSlots(role, step, share)` of
  `sdk/src/layout.ts`: slot `g * 16 + i` of ciphertext `h` is the tensor entry of the lane `8 h + g`
  at the level `i`. The circuit computes the tensor of the seller and that of the buyer, and `role`
  selects one of them with an arithmetic selection. Both ciphertexts use the same `step` and
  `share`.
- Ciphertext 2 encrypts a mask: slot values below `2^9` (the plaintext is their residue mod 257),
  and for every level `i` the sum over the 8 groups `g` of slot `g * 16 + i` is 0 mod 257. The mask
  values are private witnesses.

The SIMD plaintext is related to the `k1` of the encryption proof by a fixed linear map. The circuit
checks it with the table `circuits/lib/src/constants.nr` (generated by
`circuits/tools/src/bin/offer_table.rs` from fhe.rs):
`sum_s value_s * TABLE_UNIT[s][i] = k1[i] + 257 * q[i]` for every coefficient `i`.

The tree has two circuits that are not the EVM verifier:

| Circuit                   | Role                                                                                   | Public inputs                                                                              |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `negotiation_offer_lanes` | Two wrapper proofs (ciphertexts 0 and 1) and the lane tensor. Recursive, no ZK.        | `ct_commitments[2]`, `role`, `levels`, `pk_row0`, `ude_key_hash`, `ct0_chain`, `ct1_chain` |
| `negotiation_offer`       | The lanes proof, the wrapper proof of the mask, the mask rules. EVM verifier, with ZK. | the eight above                                                                            |

`pk_row0` is the commitment of row 0 of the committee key, which the encryption proof uses. The top
circuit rebuilds `pk_commitment` from `pk_row0`, the other key rows and the relinearization key
commitment that the client opens (`openLbfvKeyEnvelope`).

`vk_anchor = compute_vk_hash([ude_key_hash, ct0_chain, ct1_chain, lanes_key_hash])`. It covers the
user-data encryption circuits and the lanes circuit, so a proof with another circuit in any position
of the tree fails. `circuits/compile.sh` computes it and writes `circuits/target/vk_anchor.json` as
`{ "vkAnchor": "0x..." }`. The SDK ships the file.

Gate counts (`bb gates`, every circuit stays below 2^21 so that a browser can prove it):

| Circuit                   | Gates     |
| ------------------------- | --------- |
| `negotiation_offer_lanes` | 1,452,410 |
| `negotiation_offer`       | 1,431,863 |

The proof bytes do not contain the public inputs. `OfferProver.prove` returns
`{ ciphertexts, commitments, proof }`; the contract builds inputs 3 to 7 itself.

## Slot layout of the offer plaintexts

`N = 128` slots (insecure-simd, `t = 257`). Slot `g * 16 + i` is group `g` (0 to 7), level `i` (0 to
15).

| Ciphertext | Slot `g * 16 + i`                                                  |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0          | Tensor entry of the lane `g` at the level `i` (0 or 1).            |
| 1          | Tensor entry of the lane `g + 8` at the level `i` (0 or 1).        |
| 2          | Mask value (sum over `g` of the slot is 0 mod 257 for each level). |

The program outputs `X0 * Y0 + X1 * Y1 + M_seller + M_buyer` slot by slot. After the decryption, the
sum over `g` of the output slots of a level is 1 for the agreed level and 0 for every other level
(`DESIGN.md`).
